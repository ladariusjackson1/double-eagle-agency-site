Questions owners ask me

Don't see yours? Ask my AI agent, or call me at (662) 629-1077.

Will it sound like a robot?

It uses a natural, human sounding voice and talks the way a friendly front desk person would. You don't have to guess. Click "Talk to my AI now" on this page and hear it yourself.

What if it gets something wrong?

It only works from what you approve: your services, prices, hours and service area. When a question falls outside that, it takes the caller's details and passes them to you instead of guessing.

In the first weeks, I go through the conversations with you and fix anything that's off.

Do I need to change my current website?

No. If you like your site, I can add the voice agent and chat to it. If it's outdated or slow, I'll build you a new one. We'll decide together on our first call.

Can I cancel?

Yes. If you pay the $1,500 setup fee, you're month to month and can cancel any time.

If you take the 12 month plan so setup is waived, you're committing to 12 months. I'll walk you through both before you sign anything.

I'm not a tech person. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That's the reason to hire me. I set it all up and keep it running. Your part is one call to tell me about your business, then checking your phone when new leads come in.

What happens when a lead comes in?

You get a text with the person's name, number and what they need. If they booked, the appointment is already on your calendar. Every conversation is saved in your CRM, so nothing slips through.

How long does setup take?

Plan on about two weeks from our first call to going live. Most of that time goes to building and testing your agent, so it's ready before a real customer ever talks to it.

Why only 3 founding spots?

I'm new, and I want my first clients to get my full attention. Three is what I can take on at that level. You get my best price, and I get real results to show the next business owner.
