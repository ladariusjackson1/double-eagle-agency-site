Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

Double Eagle Agency ("I," "me," "my") is a one person business in North Mississippi. I build websites with AI voice agents for local service businesses. This policy explains what information I collect when you use this website, talk to my AI agent, book a call, or text with me, and what I do with it.

Information I collect

Information you give me. When you talk to my AI voice agent, use the chat, book a call, call me or send me a message, you may share your name, phone number, email address, business name, and details about your business and what you need.

Calls and conversations. Conversations with my AI voice agent and chat are processed by AI software and may be recorded and transcribed so I can follow up with you and improve how the agent answers. Please don't share sensitive personal information, like financial account numbers or health details, with the agent.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, this site and the tools on it may collect basic technical information such as your browser type, device, IP address, pages you visit and the time of your visit. The chat and voice widget and the booking calendar may use cookies or similar technology to work properly.

How I use your information

  • To answer your questions and follow up with you
  • To schedule and confirm calls and appointments
  • To send you information you asked for about my services
  • To provide services to clients and manage my client relationships
  • To improve this website and my AI agent
  • To keep the site secure and meet legal obligations

Text messages (SMS)

If you give me your mobile number and agree to receive texts, I may send you messages about your inquiry, appointment confirmations and reminders, and follow ups about my services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of buying anything.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third parties. The only exception is the service providers that deliver my text messages for me, as described below.

Who I share information with

I don't sell your personal information. I share it only with the service providers that help me run my business, and only as much as they need to do their job:

  • GoHighLevel (LeadConnector), which powers my AI voice agent, chat, booking calendar, text messaging and CRM
  • Netlify, which hosts this website
  • Google Fonts, which delivers the fonts on this site

I may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect my rights or the safety of others.

How long I keep it

I keep your information as long as I need it to work with you or follow up on your inquiry, and as long as the law requires. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

How I protect it

I use reputable providers with standard security protections and limit access to your information to me. No method of storing or sending data online is 100% secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect what you share.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete the information I have about you
  • Opt out of texts by replying STOP
  • Opt out of emails by using the unsubscribe link or replying to tell me
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings

Children

This site is meant for business owners. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
